Monthly CTC equivalent :

Meaning: The total monthly Cost to Company, including gross pay and employer-paid benefits.

Why it matters: It shows the complete financial investment your employer makes in you.

Take-home impact: It is your highest salary figure but includes non-cash components you won't see monthly.

Monthly Gross :

Meaning: Your total earnings before any taxes or PF are subtracted.

Why it matters: It is the base number banks use to check your loan eligibility.

Take-home impact: It is a starting benchmark; your actual paycheck will be lower.

Estimated Deductions :

Meaning: Money subtracted for statutory taxes (TDS, PT) and provident fund (PF).

Why it matters: It ensures tax compliance and builds your long-term retirement savings.

Take-home impact: Higher deductions directly reduce your cash-in-hand.

Estimated Monthly Take-Home :

Meaning: The actual net cash credited to your bank account each month.

Why it matters: It defines your real disposable income for daily living and saving.

Take-home impact: This is your final, liquid income after all deductions.
